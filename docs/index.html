Privacy Policy

Smart Price Drop Tracker · Last updated: 15 November 2026

Short version: Smart Price Drop Tracker is local-only. Your tracked products, price history and settings stay in your browser on your device. There is no account, no developer server, no analytics, no advertising and no sale of data.

1. Who this policy covers

This policy covers the Smart Price Drop Tracker browser extension (the “extension”), developed by Raajan (the “developer”, “we”, “us”). It explains what the extension stores, what network requests it makes, and how you can delete your data.

2. Data stored on your device

The extension handles two categories of user data, only for the products you explicitly choose to track:

  • Website content: the product page HTML fetched for a price check, plus the product title, image URL, price, MRP, availability and retailer name extracted from it. The HTML is parsed locally and is not stored; only the extracted product fields are saved.
  • Web browsing activity: the product page URLs you add to your watchlist and the timestamps of their price checks. The extension does not read or store your general browsing history.

The following information is stored in chrome.storage.local on the browser profile where you installed the extension:

  • Product names, product page URLs, product image URLs and retailer names.
  • Current price, original/MRP, target price, availability and currency.
  • Price history, price-drop statistics and check timestamps.
  • Check status, confidence values and error information for the last check.
  • Your settings: theme, default check frequency, notification preferences and display options.

Legacy temporary-tab cleanup entries may be kept in chrome.storage.session until the browser session ends. The current version does not open tabs for automatic checks.

This data is not synced to your Google account and is not sent to the developer or to any third party.

3. Network requests

The extension makes network requests only in these situations:

  • When you click the extension icon: the popup reads the current product page inside your browser using the activeTab and scripting permissions. The page content is processed locally. Nothing is uploaded to the developer.
  • When automatic price checks run: a hidden offscreen document fetches the product page URL that you chose to track. This is a normal request to the retailer’s website, so the retailer may receive your IP address, cookies for that site and standard request headers, just as if you had visited the page. The fetched HTML is parsed locally and is not stored or sent to the developer.
  • When product images are displayed: the dashboard and popup load image URLs from the retailer’s image servers. The retailer may receive the request information normally associated with loading an image.

There are no requests to a developer-operated server, no analytics or telemetry, no advertising SDKs, no third-party APIs and no remotely hosted code.

4. Permissions and why they are needed

  • storage — save your products, price history, settings and cleanup state locally.
  • alarms — schedule the 6, 12 or 24-hour price checks you choose, plus maintenance.
  • notifications — show local price-drop and target-reached alerts.
  • activeTab — read the current page only after you click the extension icon.
  • scripting — run the bundled read-only extraction script in the current tab after you click the icon.
  • offscreen — fetch and parse a monitored product page in a hidden document so scheduled checks do not open tabs.
  • Optional host permissions — requested for one exact store origin only when you enable automatic checks for a product. Nothing is granted at install time. You can remove access later in the extension’s Settings page or in your browser’s extension settings.

5. Sharing and sale of data

We do not sell, rent, trade or share your data. We do not receive your tracked products, price history, browsing activity or page content. The extension has no account system and no backend.

6. Limited Use disclosure

The extension’s use of user data is limited to providing and improving its single purpose: tracking the prices of products the user explicitly chooses and showing price history, targets and alerts. User data is not used or transferred for personalised advertising, profiling, resale or any unrelated purpose. The developer does not permit humans to read user data except when required by law, when necessary for security, or when the user gives specific consent for support. This disclosure is intended to meet the Chrome Web Store User Data Policy and Limited Use requirements.

7. Data retention and deletion

  • Removing a product deletes that product’s record and price history from local storage.
  • Clearing the extension’s storage or uninstalling the extension deletes all extension data.
  • Exported JSON/CSV backup files are created only when you request them and are saved wherever your browser saves downloads. You control those files.
  • Retailer websites and browser caches may retain request logs under their own policies; the developer does not control those systems.

8. Security

Data is stored in your browser’s extension storage, which is protected by the browser’s own security model. No method of storage is completely secure. Keep your browser and operating system up to date, and protect access to your device and browser profile.

9. Children

The extension is a general-purpose shopping utility and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

10. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the “Last updated” date will change and the new version will be published at the same URL. Material changes will also be noted in the extension’s store listing or release notes.
